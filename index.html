South African Social Security Agency (SASSA) SRD R370 Updates

Providing transparent guidelines and timely status checks for South African beneficiaries.

The Department of Social Development and SASSA adjusted the COVID-19 Social Relief of Distress (SRD) grant from R350 to R370 per month. Eligible citizens, permanent residents, and permit holders who have no substantial financial income rely on this lifeline monthly support.

Official Reference Portal

For continuous application guidelines, regulations, and announcements regarding social grants, you can visit the primary resource directly at https://srdgrant.org.za/.

Beneficiaries are strongly advised to perform regular monthly reviews on their application to prevent payment interruptions or bank detail discrepancies. You can instantly run your online sassa status check to confirm whether your grant for the current cycle is approved, pending bank details, or undergoing identity verification.

When tracking your payout status, always verify that your banking details are matched under your legal name. SASSA conducts monthly bank verification checks to ensure income does not surpass the means-test threshold of R625 per month.

Common SASSA Status Meanings

Application Approved

Your grant has been authorized for payout. A payment date will be scheduled and sent to your bank or cash-send collection partner.

Pending Verification

Your application was received and is undergoing identity or financial means testing verification across government and financial databases.

Application Declined

Income over the statutory threshold or alternative sources (e.g., UIF, NSFAS, or employment) were detected. You have 30 days to lodge an appeal.

Who Qualifies for R370?

  • ✓ South African Citizens, Permanent Residents, or Refugee Permit holders.
  • ✓ Individuals between 18 and 59 years of age.
  • ✓ Insufficient income (monthly bank deposits below R625).
  • ✓ Not receiving alternative social grants or government support (UIF / NSFAS).

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an appeal take?

Appeals lodged to the Independent Tribunal (ITAC) usually take between 60 to 90 days to receive a final determination.

Can I change my phone number or bank details?

Yes. You can update your payment details on the official SRD portal at any time. Verification takes roughly 5 to 7 working days.
